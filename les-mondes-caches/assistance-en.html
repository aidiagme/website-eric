Support

A hint to keep going.

Help with the three worlds, local progress and contacting the publisher.

How do I play?

  1. Choose Halloween, Christmas or Easter and open the theme’s first free scene.
  2. Pinch or use + and − to zoom. Drag the illustration to explore its details.
  3. Tap the hidden witch, Santa or chocolate bunnies. Easter scenes require finding every bunny.
  4. Use an optional hint when needed. There is no timer.
  5. Return to the catalogue to pick a scene. Replaying resets only the selected scene.

Common questions

Which scenes are available?

The first scene of each theme is free: three scenes in total. The other nine scenes are reserved for the planned complete pack, without a subscription. In the current version, purchasing and restoration are unavailable and those nine scenes stay locked.

Why can’t I buy or restore the pack?

Payment and restoration are disabled in this version. The game may query the App Store or Google Play for store availability and the pack’s price; this does not enable payment. No purchase proof is sent to the publisher’s verification service.

When purchasing becomes available, the price offered will be the one displayed by your store, in your currency. Changing the game’s language does not convert this price.

Do I need an account or Internet?

No game account is required. The illustrations are bundled with the app and the free scenes can be played offline. Checking store availability and the price may use the Internet. Progress does not sync between devices.

How do I change language?

Use the game’s language setting to select French, English, Spanish, German, Italian or Portuguese. The selection stays on your device and does not change progress.

How can I delete my local data?

On Android, open Settings, Apps, Les mondes cachés, Storage, Clear data. Labels vary by device. On iPhone or iPad, use Delete App rather than Offload App. This may erase progress.

Manage system backups separately: restoring a backup may bring older data back. Read more about deletion.
