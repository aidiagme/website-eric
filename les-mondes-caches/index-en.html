An observation game for the family

Three celebrations.
Well-hidden characters.

Explore seasonal illustrations, zoom into the details and take your time searching.

Three free scenes, nine more with one purchase. No subscription, no ads.

01 · Halloween

Where is the witch?

A village, a forest, a greenhouse and a castle: find the witch in every illustration.

02 · Christmas

Find Santa.

From the snowy market to the great Christmas tree, look for Santa in four festive scenes.

03 · Easter

Look for the bunnies.

Find every chocolate bunny: two, three, four then five, depending on the scene.

At your own pace.

Pinch to zoom, pan the illustration and tap the hidden characters. You can also zoom with the + and − buttons. Optional hints help you search, with no timer.

The game relies on observing the illustrations. It is intended for families, including children.

Three free scenes.

The first scene of each theme is free. The catalogue contains twelve scenes in total. A one-time purchase, without a subscription, unlocks all nine additional scenes across the three themes.

The price is the one displayed by your store, in your currency. A parental gate protects the purchase, and you can restore the pack on your other devices.

Pick up where you left off on your device.

Progress and the chosen language are stored locally. No game account is required. The game contains no advertising or audience analytics tool.

The illustrations are bundled with the app. The interface and content are available in French, English, Spanish, German, Italian and Portuguese.
