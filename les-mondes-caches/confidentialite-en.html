Privacy

Your progress and game data.

Progress stays on your device. Here is how the game, store queries and support work.

Scope: version 1.0.0, with purchasing and restoration disabled.
Policy updated on 18 October 2026.

1. Publisher and scope

Eric Benzakin publishes Les mondes cachés / Hidden Worlds. Contact: 2bforce06@gmail.com. This policy covers the game version described above, voluntary support messages and these information pages.

The game is intended for families, including children. It does not require a game account or identity information to play. A parent or guardian can contact support for a child.

2. Progress and language on your device

The game stores found targets, viewed hints, completed scenes and your chosen language on your device. This information lets you resume playing. The game uses no publisher server for synchronizing progress.

Your progress and language information are not sent to a publisher server. System backups may retain them depending on your settings.

3. Exchanges with stores

The game may query the App Store or Google Play for store availability and the pack’s price. Those services process these exchanges under their own policies: Apple’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

In this version, payment and restoration are disabled. The game does not call the publisher’s purchase-verification service or send it any purchase proof. This policy will be updated before purchasing, restoration or that service is enabled.

4. Advertising and analytics

The game contains no advertising or audience analytics tool.

5. Delete local data

Progress and language are kept in local app data. On Android, clear the app’s data in storage settings. On iPhone or iPad, use Delete App rather than Offload App. This may erase progress.

Manage system backups separately: restoring a backup may bring older data back. The publisher does not synchronize progress across devices.

6. Support messages

If you voluntarily email support, the publisher receives your email address, any sender name, message and attachments that you choose to send. These are used to handle and follow up your request and retained as needed for that follow-up.

You can use 2bforce06@gmail.com for questions or deletion requests. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens or raw receipts. Avoid personal information that is unnecessary for your request.

7. Information pages

These pages load no scripts, analytics tools, advertising trackers or remote fonts. They contain no forms and add no application cookies.

For visits on aidiagme.github.io, GitHub Pages states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged for security. See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub privacy policy for this hosting-related processing.

8. Questions and updates

Contact 2bforce06@gmail.com about processing by Eric Benzakin for the game or its support. The policy’s date and scope appear at the top of this page. Changes to data-processing features, including enabling purchases and purchase verification, must be accompanied by updated information.
