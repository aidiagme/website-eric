Privacy

Your progress and game data.

Progress stays on your device. Here is how the game, store queries and support work.

Scope: version 1.0.0 of the game on iPhone, iPad and Android, including the one-time purchase of the complete pack, purchase restoration and verification.
Policy updated on 19 October 2026.

1. Publisher and scope

Eric Benzakin publishes Les mondes cachés / Hidden Worlds. Contact: 2bforce06@gmail.com. This policy covers the game version described above, voluntary support messages and these information pages.

The game is intended for families, including children. It does not require a game account or identity information to play. A parent or guardian can contact support for a child.

2. Progress and language on your device

The game stores found targets, viewed hints, completed scenes and your chosen language on your device. This information lets you resume playing. The game uses no publisher server for synchronizing progress.

Your progress and language information are not sent to a publisher server. System backups may retain them depending on your settings.

3. Pack purchase and stores

Three scenes are free. The other nine are unlocked with a one-time purchase, without a subscription, made through the App Store or Google Play. Before any purchase, a parental gate asks you to solve a small sum. The game queries the store to display the pack’s price in your currency.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own policies: Apple’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy. The game and the publisher’s verification service never receive your payment-card details or the email address or name of your store account.

4. Purchase verification

The game may automatically check the verification service’s availability, including on startup or resume, even without starting a purchase. This request contains no purchase proof but produces the connection metadata described in the next section.

For a recognized purchase or restoration event, the game sends five items to the service: the platform (iOS or Android), application identifier, pack identifier, transaction identifier and verification proof supplied by the store (an Apple-signed transaction or a Google purchase token). These items are used to check the purchase’s status with Apple or Google before recognizing entitlement to the pack. A purchase or restoration already initiated may be processed when the store confirmation arrives, without another tap on a purchase button.

Progress and the chosen language are not sent to the verifier. Its code creates no purchase database and does not write purchase proofs or tokens to its logs.

5. Verifier hosting and technical logs

The verification service is hosted on Google Cloud Run in the europe-west1 region in Belgium. Google Cloud creates request logs for operating, securing and diagnosing the service. They may contain the client IP address, client type (user-agent), date and time, requested path, response status and technical information such as the server IP address, protocol, exchange sizes and duration. These logs may be created by an automatic availability check without a purchase.

Request logs are configured for retention of 30 days. Google Cloud administrative audit logs are retained for 400 days; this does not mean that purchase proofs are retained for 400 days. Log storage is configured with a “global” location and is not guaranteed to remain exclusively within the European Union. These platform logs are distinct from the proofs processed by the verification code.

6. Advertising and analytics

The game contains no advertising or audience analytics tool.

7. Delete local data

Progress and language are kept in local app data. On Android, clear the app’s data in storage settings. On iPhone or iPad, use Delete App rather than Offload App. This may erase progress.

Manage system backups separately: restoring a backup may bring older data back. The publisher does not synchronize progress across devices.

Deleting the game or its local data does not delete transactions retained by Apple or Google, or technical logs already created by the service. The store’s tools and rules apply to its transactions; you can contact the publisher with questions about processing it performs.

8. Support messages

If you voluntarily email support, the publisher receives your email address, any sender name, message and attachments that you choose to send. These are used to handle and follow up your request and retained as needed for that follow-up.

You can use 2bforce06@gmail.com for questions or deletion requests. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens or raw receipts. Avoid personal information that is unnecessary for your request.

9. Information pages

These pages load no scripts, analytics tools, advertising trackers or remote fonts. They contain no forms and add no application cookies.

For visits on aidiagme.github.io, GitHub Pages states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged for security. See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub privacy policy for this hosting-related processing.

10. Questions and updates

Contact 2bforce06@gmail.com about processing by Eric Benzakin for the game or its support. The policy’s date and scope appear at the top of this page. This information will be updated when the described processing changes.
